Context & challenge

Strax is an express grocery concept designed for people in central Stockholm who need groceries quickly. Orders are packed at nearby dark stores and delivered within 10 minutes, focusing on immediate grocery needs rather than a traditional weekly shop.

Business constraint

150 SEK break-even order value. The experience needed to keep quick purchases simple while still creating opportunities for users to add more to their basket.

Five core use cases
01 Spontaneous dinner — Ingredients for tonight's meal
02 Dinner planning — Stocking up when there's no time to shop
03 Household emergency — Diapers, toilet paper, can't wait
04 Movie night — Snacks and drinks without leaving home
05 Weekend breakfast — Something extra for a spontaneous breakfast
02

Starting point

I didn't start from a blank page. The course provided user research and low-fidelity wireframes covering the core experience, including delivery availability, product browsing, and the cart.

My task was to build on this foundation and develop it into a high-fidelity MVP, refining and extending the experience where needed.

What I'd do differently

The biggest thing I'd change is when I started validating my decisions.

I spent most of the project moving from the provided research and wireframes into a polished UI, while making assumptions about what would help users act quickly and what could encourage a higher basket value.

If I continued the project, I'd test those assumptions earlier with a clickable prototype — especially the recipe-to-cart flow, product bundles, and the 150 SEK threshold in the cart. I'd want to see whether they actually help users complete an urgent shop without adding unnecessary friction.

I'd also involve a developer earlier when defining components and responsive behaviour. I created the design system to make the product more consistent and easier to hand off, but reviewing it together with a developer would help me understand which patterns are genuinely reusable and where the system needs to be more flexible.
