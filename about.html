About me

Hi, I'm Sunghye. I'm studying UX design at Nackademin in Solna.

Before UX, I worked in logistics, coordinating shipments from Asia to the Nordics. It was a job that involved a lot of moving parts, different people, and unexpected problems. I enjoyed figuring things out and finding ways to make the process smoother.

That interest in solving problems eventually led me to UX design. I like asking questions, looking at things from different perspectives, and understanding why something works for one person but not another.
