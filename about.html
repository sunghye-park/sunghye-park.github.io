About me

Hi, I'm Sunghye. I'm studying UX design at Nackademin in Solna.

Before UX, I worked in logistics, coordinating shipments from Asia to the Nordics. Things didn't always go as planned, and there were often time-sensitive problems to deal with. I enjoyed figuring things out and finding ways to get things back on track.

I've always felt that things are easier to figure out when you understand where the other person is coming from. Working in logistics gave me plenty of chances to see that in practice. Now, as I study UX, I'm getting to explore that idea in a different way.
